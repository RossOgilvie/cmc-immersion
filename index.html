About

The spectral curve is ν² = λ a(λ) with a(λ) = −∏(λ − αᵢ)(1 − ᾱᵢλ), of genus g = number of branch points αᵢ. A polynomial Killing field ζ(λ) with −det ζ = a/λ is integrated along the z-plane together with the extended frame F and ∂λF at the Sym point λ₀. The Sym–Bobenko formula f = 4 ∂θF F⁻¹ then gives the immersion with H = ½ and metric 4e²ᵘ|dz|²; the H slider rescales it by 1/(2H).

Genus 1: the dashed diameter through λ₀ is where the surface closes into a Delaunay cylinder (unduloid on the far side, nodoid on the near side); branch points snap onto it. A double branch point α₁ = α₂ = λ₀(k − √(k²−1))², k ≥ 2 (a resonance point of the cylinder) gives a k-lobed bubbleton; close up finds the periods of whatever is on screen.

Moving λ₀ alone traverses the associated family. For g ≥ 3 the sliders move along the isospectral torus. For g ≤ 2 every isospectral deformation is a translation of the domain. Periods and closing conditions are not imposed: these are immersed planes, which close up into cylinders or tori only for special data.

Method: Pinkall–Sterling (1989); Bobenko (1991); conventions of Kilian–Schmidt and Burstall–Ferus–Pedit–Pinkall. See cmc_spectral_to_immersion.md.
